# Bottom-hardness derivation contract

This product has no independent remote source. The rock-presence grid and sediment
texture grids have no verified shared areal denominator in the pinned provider
version. The former weighted `BOTTOM_HARDNESS_INDEX` is therefore **unavailable**
under `hardness_unavailable_unverified_joint_denominator_v2`, as are consolidated
substrate and modeled hard-substrate area fractions. `DERIVATION_QC_REASON` explains
why. The separate modeled rock-presence score is carried through for inspection,
without relabeling it as measured consolidation, acoustic backscatter, areal rock
cover, or a calibrated hardness probability. Unsupported boulder, cobble, and mixed
classes do not contribute invented zeros. Old releases retain the historical index
and require version-aware interpretation.
